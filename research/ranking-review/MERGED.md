# The merged ranking

One timeline built from [ranking-sonnet.md](ranking-sonnet.md), [ranking-sol.md](ranking-sol.md) and [ranking-neon.md](ranking-neon.md), using the method and the owner decisions in [COMPARISON.md](COMPARISON.md). It is the SNAPSHOTS table in `index.html`; [merged.txt](merged.txt) is a copy of it. The final picture is in [merged-final.png](merged-final.png).

**31 entries:** the opening state, 29 events and the closing standings, five cars each.

## The rules

1. **Events:** kept when at least two of the three rankings include them. Dates are the day the model became publicly usable.
2. **Order:** the one at least two rankings give. Where all three differ, the owner decisions apply; failing that, the middle ranking.
3. **Gaps:** for each car the median of the three rankings' gaps, rounded to 0.05. A ranking that lacks the event counts with its standings at that time.
4. **Owner decisions:** DeepSeek R1 enters second (20 Jan 2025). Anthropic leads from GPT-5.2's week (11 Dec 2025). Moonshot is fifth at the end, and SpaceX AI is out after the index v5 re-rating (27 Oct 2026).

## Judgement calls beyond the rules

1. **Kimi K3.1 Pro (23 Oct 2026) is folded into the index v5 step (27 Oct).** Sonnet and Sol both have a 23 Oct step, and by the rules it would put Moonshot fifth and SpaceX AI out four days before v5. The owner decision says SpaceX AI goes out after v5, and COMPARISON.md lists "the index v5 re-rating, or Kimi K3.1 Pro" as one moment. So there is one step, on 27 Oct, marked as Moonshot's.
2. **Fable 5.2 (9 Nov) and Carbon going public (10 Nov) are two steps.** Sol has both; Sonnet shows them as one snapshot on 10 Nov; Neon has only 9 Nov. On 9 Nov two rankings keep OpenAI second, and on 10 Nov two put Google second, so both steps follow the rules.
3. **DeepSeek stays second on 17 Feb 2025 (Grok 3).** Two rankings have Anthropic ahead of DeepSeek that day, but they are the same two the owner overruled on 20 Jan, and nothing changed for Anthropic until Claude 3.7 a week later. From 24 Feb the two-of-three order (Anthropic second, DeepSeek third) applies.
4. **Grok 3 enters fifth (17 Feb 2025).** The three differ: third (Sol), fifth (Neon), not yet in (Sonnet, which has it fifth a week later). Fifth is the middle.
5. **GPT-5.4 (5 Mar 2026) and Muse Spark (8 Apr 2026): Anthropic, OpenAI, Google.** No two rankings give the same front three. Taken pair by pair, two of three put Anthropic ahead of both, and two of three put OpenAI ahead of Google. That is Neon's order.
6. **Opus 4.8 (28 May 2026): SpaceX AI fourth, Meta fifth.** Sonnet and Neon disagree on that pair, and Sol has no step that day. Sol's next step (9 Jun) has SpaceX AI ahead, which settles 9 Jun by two of three. Using the same order on 28 May avoids the two cars swapping at Fable 5's launch for no reason.
7. **Four steps use the median by position instead of by car** for part of the field, because the rankings had those cars in different places and their gaps were measured to different cars: the back pair on 28 May, 9 Jun and 12 Jun 2026 (SpaceX AI and Meta), and OpenAI and Google on 9 Nov 2026 (Sonnet's snapshot there already has Carbon public).
8. **One gap is moved so the step can be driven.** On 24 Feb 2025 Google is at +0.45, not the median +0.35. With +0.35 the next step (Gemini 2.5 Pro going from fourth to first) could not be driven cleanly on desktop: `frontierLap.check()` reported 25 tight samples at 1440x900. With +0.45 it passes at all nine tested screen sizes.
9. **Dates where the rankings differ by a few days:** Claude 4 on 22 May 2025 (Sol: 28 May, with R1-0528), GPT-5.6 Sol on 9 Jul 2026 (Sol: 10 Jul), GPT-6 Astra on 3 Sep 2026 (Sol: 7 Sep, after the re-rating), index v5 on 27 Oct 2026 (Sonnet: 28 Oct, with Carbon). The closing state stays on 20 Nov 2026.
10. **The closing step is marked as OpenAI's.** Sol and Neon mark Google (Neon was the last release, on 18 Nov), Sonnet marks OpenAI. The move in the step is OpenAI retaking second, so its car is the one that lights up.
11. **A returning lab's note starts with "BACK:", a first-time lab's with "NEW:".** NEW is used for DeepSeek and for Moonshot's first entry; the eight returns (xAI twice, Meta twice, Moonshot three times, Google once) say BACK.
12. **The step for GPT-5.2 is marked as OpenAI's although Anthropic takes the lead in it.** It is OpenAI's release; the owner decision is about who leads after it.
