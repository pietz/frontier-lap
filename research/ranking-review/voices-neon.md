# Phase 1: Who to Listen To (Nov 2024 – Nov 2026)

Informed voices and independent evaluators to layer onto the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index across Nov 2024 – Nov 2026 (starting with the three requested in the brief).

## Owner-Named Voices

1. **Every (Dan Shipper, Alex Duffy & team)** — SF media collective and AI app studio (Spiral, Cora, Sparkle).
   - **Publishes:** [every.to/vibe-check](https://every.to/vibe-check), [every.to/chain-of-thought](https://every.to/chain-of-thought), X.
   - **Good at:** Writing taste, daily-driver UX, real-world coding/agentic workflows, strategic steerability (*AI Diplomacy*).
   - **Lean / COI:** Receives NDA early access from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google and builds on their APIs (historically Claude-leaning on writing/Claude Code). Shipper holds minor angel stakes in AI tooling (e.g., Stainless), no disclosed lab equity; team publishes blunt negative reviews.
   - **Trust: 4/5** — Best structured release-day gauge of practical "feel," discounted slightly for cozy US-lab preview access.

2. **Theo Browne (t3.gg / Ping Labs)** — Dev YouTuber and CEO of YC-backed Ping Labs (T3 Chat, T3 Code).
   - **Publishes:** [YouTube (@t3dotgg)](https://www.youtube.com/@t3dotgg), [t3.gg](https://t3.gg/), X, [SnitchBench](https://snitchbench.t3.gg/).
   - **Good at:** Web/TypeScript coding, coding-agent harnesses, latency/cost value, refusal quirks (SnitchBench, SkateBench).
   - **Lean / COI:** Disclosed YouTube tech sponsors and commercial multi-model apps; no lab equity. Openly admits taste bias and occasional early access; favors loud, contrarian developer takes.
   - **Trust: 4/5** — High-signal coding-agent and value practitioner once you filter out YouTube theatricality.

3. **Matt Shumer** — CEO of HyperWrite (OthersideAI), AI startup investor, *Something Big* publisher.
   - **Publishes:** X ([@mattshumer_](https://x.com/mattshumer_)), [shumer.dev](https://shumer.dev/), [somethingbig.ai](https://somethingbig.ai/).
   - **Good at:** Mainstream retail hype waves — not separating frontier models.
   - **Lean / COI:** Severe credibility hit from the Sep 2024 **Reflection 70B** scandal (unreproducible benchmark claims, "Claude in a trench coat" allegations, undisclosed stake in Glaive AI) plus founder/investor exposure to AI hype; his Feb 2026 viral essay (*Something Big Is Happening*) drew wide pushback for sensationalism.
   - **Trust: 1/5** — Useful only as a retail-hype barometer, never for frontier ordering.

## Independent Practitioners & Technical Analysts

4. **Simon Willison** — Independent open-source developer (Datasette, `llm` CLI).
   - **Publishes:** [simonwillison.net](https://simonwillison.net/).
   - **Good at:** Day-one coding, tool use, open-weight/local models, value, quick multimodal/SVG sanity checks ("pelican on a bicycle").
   - **Lean / COI:** No lab equity or employment; added weekly banner sponsors in Feb 2026 under strict editorial separation and a public [disclosures policy](https://simonwillison.net/about/#disclosures).
   - **Trust: 5/5** — Most disciplined, conflict-free day-one practitioner log across the entire two-year window.

5. **Nathan Lambert** — Post-training researcher; led OLMo/Tülu at AI2 until June 2026, then co-founded nonprofit Trillium Labs.
   - **Publishes:** [interconnects.ai](https://www.interconnects.ai/), X.
   - **Good at:** Raw reasoning/RL post-training mechanics and vetting open-weight/Chinese labs (DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, Llama) vs. closed labs.
   - **Lean / COI:** First-party insider on AI2's OLMo/Tülu releases (treat as first-party) and explicitly pro–open science; financially independent of commercial frontier labs.
   - **Trust: 5/5** — Unmatched technical lens for distinguishing real reasoning gains from benchmark polish, especially on Chinese open weights.

6. **Zvi Mowshowitz** — Independent analyst, mathematician, former quant/game designer.
   - **Publishes:** *Don't Worry About the Vase* ([thezvi.substack.com](https://thezvi.substack.com/)).
   - **Good at:** Launch-week power-user consensus, writing quality, sycophancy/personality regressions, raw reasoning synthesis.
   - **Lean / COI:** No lab funding or equity; strong AI-safety/x-risk lens makes him harsh on lab governance, though capability verdicts stay clean.
   - **Trust: 4/5** — Best single historical archive of what serious practitioners actually observed on release week.

7. **Ethan Mollick** — Wharton professor, co-director of Wharton Generative AI Labs.
   - **Publishes:** [oneusefulthing.org](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/), X.
   - **Good at:** Non-coding knowledge work, writing, research synthesis, everyday "jagged frontier" usability.
   - **Lean / COI:** Unfunded by AI labs, but routinely receives disclosed NDA early preview access from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google; leans mildly optimistic.
   - **Trust: 4/5** — Essential counterweight to coder-only reviews so general knowledge-work strength counts.

8. **Andrej Karpathy** — Ex-OpenAI co-founder, ex-Tesla AI director; ran Eureka Labs (2024–May 2026) before **joining Anthropic's pre-training team on May 19, 2026**.
   - **Publishes:** X ([@karpathy](https://x.com/karpathy)), GitHub (*LLM Council*).
   - **Good at:** Coding workflow shifts ("vibe coding"), multi-model ensembling, engineering model feel.
   - **Lean / COI:** Ex-OpenAI co-founder; **direct Anthropic employee from May 2026 onward**, compromising neutrality on late-2026 rankings.
   - **Trust: 3/5** — 5/5 engineering taste through Apr 2026, then must be discounted as an Anthropic insider.

## Independent Evaluators & Benchmarks

9. **METR (Model Evaluation & Threat Research)** — Nonprofit conducting pre-deployment frontier evals (*Time Horizons*; co-built *MirrorCode* with Epoch AI).
   - **Publishes:** [metr.org](https://metr.org/), [Time Horizons](https://metr.org/blog/measuring-ai-ability-time-horizons/).
   - **Good at:** Long-horizon autonomous coding/agentic tasks and transcript auditing to catch reward hacking.
   - **Lean / COI:** Hard rule refusing frontier-lab funding (philanthropically backed, including $71M in Aug 2026); relies on lab cooperation for pre-deployment access, with independent reporting.
   - **Trust: 5/5** — Gold standard for multi-hour autonomous agent capability with the strongest independence firewall in the field.

10. **Terminal-Bench (Laude Institute & Stanford)** — Open-source containerized terminal agent benchmark and *Harbor* harness (Andy Konwinski, Ludwig Schmidt, et al.).
    - **Publishes:** [tbench.ai](https://tbench.ai/).
    - **Good at:** Grounded, end-state-verified CLI, DevOps, debugging, and coding-agent execution.
    - **Lean / COI:** Academic/nonprofit governance with open Docker tasks; major labs appear among general sponsors, but none controls scoring or governance.
    - **Trust: 5/5** — Most reproducible yardstick for whether a coding model actually executes real terminal engineering work in 2025–2026.

11. **Aider Polyglot Leaderboard (Paul Gauthier)** — Independent open-source coding tool and multi-language code-editing benchmark.
    - **Publishes:** [aider.chat/docs/leaderboards](https://aider.chat/docs/leaderboards/).
    - **Good at:** Repository-level code editing across languages and cost-vs-accuracy value.
    - **Lean / COI:** Solo independent maintainer with no lab backing; bandwidth limits caused coverage lags on newer launches by mid-2026.
    - **Trust: 4/5** — Rock-solid coding/value signal across late 2024–2025; check update coverage for mid/late 2026 models.

12. **Epoch AI** — Independent institute publishing the *Epoch Capabilities Index* (ECI) and *FrontierMath*.
    - **Publishes:** [epoch.ai](https://epoch.ai/).
    - **Good at:** Raw math/STEM reasoning and longitudinal cross-benchmark capability tracking.
    - **Lean / COI:** Jan 2025 scandal after OpenAI funded *FrontierMath* under NDA with access to most problems/solutions; later added explicit disclosures, private holdout sets, and 2026 audits.
    - **Trust: 4/5** — Rigorous longitudinal tracking and post-scandal holdouts, but discount pre-holdout *FrontierMath* scores.

13. **LMArena (Arena Intelligence Inc.)** — Crowdsourced blind pairwise Elo leaderboard (formerly UC Berkeley's LMSYS Chatbot Arena).
    - **Publishes:** [arena.ai](https://arena.ai/) / [lmarena.ai](https://lmarena.ai/).
    - **Good at:** Large-scale crowdsourced conversational style and "vibe."
    - **Lean / COI:** Spun out as a for-profit VC-backed startup in Apr 2025 (~$450M raised from a16z, Felicis, UC Investments, etc., selling private evals to labs). Damaged by Meta's Apr 2025 Llama 4 Maverick bait-and-switch (#2 experimental build vs. #32 public weights) and [*The Leaderboard Illusion*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.20879) (NeurIPS 2025) documenting private variant testing and selective disclosure.
    - **Trust: 2/5** — Noisy popularity/style barometer only; too gamed and sycophancy-biased to trust on frontier order.

## Recommended Top 6

1. **METR (5/5)** — Lab-unfunded gold standard for true multi-hour autonomous agent capability.
2. **Terminal-Bench (5/5)** — Reproducible, end-state-verified yardstick for real coding and CLI agent execution.
3. **Simon Willison (5/5)** — Most consistent, conflict-free day-one developer log across coding, tool use, open weights, and value.
4. **Nathan Lambert / *Interconnects* (5/5)** — Deepest technical lens on post-training, reasoning leaps, and vetting Chinese/open-weight contenders.
5. **Every / *Vibe Check* (4/5)** — Sharpest release-day test of writing quality, "feel," and daily-driver utility when indexes miss practical friction.
6. **Zvi Mowshowitz / *Don't Worry About the Vase* (4/5)** — Best launch-week synthesis of power-user consensus, sycophancy/alignment quirks, and practitioner splits from benchmarks.
