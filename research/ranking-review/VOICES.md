# Shared shortlist: whose opinions to weigh

This merges [voices-sonnet.md](voices-sonnet.md), [voices-sol.md](voices-sol.md) and [voices-neon.md](voices-neon.md). All three rankings use this one list, so their differences come from judgement, not from different sources. Each source file has the details and links.

**The backbone stays the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index**, in the version that applied on each date. The voices below adjust it.

## Core: weigh these first

| Voice | Best at judging | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| **Simon Willison** (simonwillison.net) | what shipped, who could actually use it, reproducible hands-on tests | gets free credits and previews from every lab, and discloses them |
| **Every** (Vibe Check, Dan Shipper) | release-day team tests: writing, coding, daily use | gets early access; one investor is an early OpenAI backer; mild Anthropic and access tilt |
| **Nathan Lambert** (Interconnects) | open weights and Chinese labs (DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen), reasoning gains vs benchmark polish | leans towards open models by design |
| **METR** | how long and how reliably agents hold up on long software tasks | slow; its public tracker stopped updating in Sep 2026 |
| **Zvi Mowshowitz** (Don't Worry About the Vase) | launch-week roundups of what power users say; system-card caveats | aggregator, not a tester |
| **Theo Browne** (t3.gg, YouTube, X) | coding agents on real projects, speed, value | vibes-heavy tier lists; sells a multi-model product; has sponsors |

## Supporting: tie-breakers and cross-checks

- **Ethan Mollick** (One Useful Thing): research and other knowledge work
- **Epoch AI**: a second index to cross-check Artificial Analysis when its methodology changes. Discount FrontierMath, which OpenAI funded.
- **Terminal-Bench** and **Aider Polyglot**: reproducible coding yardsticks. Aider is strongest for 2024–25 and lags on late-2026 models.
- **Vals AI**: professional agent tasks (legal, finance, spreadsheets)
- **AI Explained**: a private reasoning benchmark
- **Arena / LMArena**: user preference in chat. A weak tie-breaker only, since the scores are style-biased and have been gamed.

## Handle with care

- **Matt Shumer:** the three agents rated him 1, 2 and 3 out of 5, because of the Reflection 70B affair (Sep 2024) and his hype-leaning essays. Use him as a pulse of heavy users only. He never moves a rank on his own.
- **Andrej Karpathy:** excellent taste, but according to one agent he joined Anthropic in May 2026, so treat his later takes as an insider's. Verify that date before relying on it.
