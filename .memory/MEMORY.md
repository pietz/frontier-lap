# Fastest Lab: project memory

- **index.html must stay one self-contained file.** The owner previews it in a viewer that opens index.html alone, so sibling files such as data.js never load. In Nov 2026 a separate data.js broke the page ("no usable snapshots"). The data now lives in the `<script id="race-data">` block near the top of index.html. Test changes with index.html copied alone into an empty folder.
- **This worktree is v2 (branch `v2-events`).** ../fastest-lab is `main`, tagged v1-time-based, the owner's frozen favourite: never touch it from here.
- **Editing the race:** change the SNAPSHOTS text table in the DATA block. `gap` is seconds to the car directly ahead. The owner's scale: 1.0 = clear lead, 0.25 = wheel to wheel, 1.7 = big break. Labs and their rebrands go in LABS (`changes: [{from, name, sub, logo}]`), and logo SVG paths go in LOGOS.
- **Event syntax (v2), both optional per snapshot:** `@lab` at the very end of the date line marks whose event the step is (its car glows, its label lights up; the lab must be in LABS and on track in that snapshot or the one before). A line `> caption` right under the date line is the commentary shown in the lower third above the timeline; a second `>` line continues it. Object form: `event: 'lab'`, `caption: '…'`. Mistakes show the yellow warning and a console message that names the snapshot. The table sits in a JS template literal: no backticks, no dollar-curly.
- **Labels (v2)** show only position, logo, name and the note (two lines on desktop, three on phones). The owner asked for the DRS / FASTEST LAB / NEW / OUT chips and all label numbers (gap to leader, INT) to go; the big interval numbers on the road stay. A joining lab is announced by its note text; a retiring one gets "Drops out of the fight." `sub` in LABS is no longer shown.
- **Caption height costs field height.** layout() measures the tallest caption at the current width and ends the field above it, so one very long caption makes every car a bit smaller. Under about 110 characters keeps it to two lines on desktop and three on a phone.
- **Knobs:** the TUNING and PLAYBACK blocks just below DATA.
  - `PLAYBACK.spacing`: `'events'` (default since v2) gives every step `PLAYBACK.event` = [2.5, 3.0] seconds (quiet step, busy step; move + hold, the last `hold[0]` = 1.1 s at rest) and an evenly spaced timeline with one mark per event; `length` and `transition` are ignored. `'calendar'` and `'uniform'` are the v1 behaviour: `length` 46 s of driving shared by calendar time or evenly, `transition` [1.7, 2.8], `hold` [1.1, 4], calendar timeline.
  - `TUNING.wobble` (1) is how far the cars drift and surge, `TUNING.pace` (1) how fast. `TUNING.clearance` (0.09 car lengths) is the least road ever left between two cars. `sideGap` (0.50 car widths, was 0.40 in v1) and `noseGap` (0.25 car lengths) are the room the plan keeps; `zoom` (1.75) stretches tight fields to fill the height.
  - `?speed=2` speeds up the replay. GT is the only car style since Nov 2026 (F1 and toy cars, the C key and `?cars=` are gone).
- **How the wobble stays safe:** `elbowRoom()` cuts every car's drift, surge and nose turn pair by pair so that two cars leaning towards each other at once still leave `clearance`. It depends only on where the plan puts the cars, so raising `wobble` or `pace` can never make cars touch; it only changes how lively free cars look. `fastestLab.check()` reports `worst` (least room over the whole timeline with the wobble at its worst) next to `clearance`; `ok: true` needs `worst >= clearance` and `tight: 0`.
- **Timeline marks in events mode:** dates under the rail are placed by priority and only where they fit: the two ends ("NOV 24"), then the year where one begins ("2025"), then the month where a quarter begins ("APR"). A year label right next to an end label is dropped; that is expected.
- **How the cars move (the drive plan):** nothing is simulated while it plays. `buildDrive()` works out, per screen size, a seat for every car at every snapshot and a path for every step between snapshots; a car's position is a pure function of the timeline position. The rule that makes "never more than three abreast" structural: two cars closer than a car length + noseGap along the road are at least a car width + sideGap apart across it, and the road is only three such lanes wide. The tightest four-car pack on the timeline caps the car size. Steps are planned in the background a few ms at a time (about 1 s in all on a laptop). `fastestLab.check()` in the console forces and measures every step; `ok: true` and `tight: 0` everywhere is the pass. New snapshots that pack four cars very closely make all cars smaller; a step that can't be driven cleanly is named in a console warning. The seats are chosen starting from the grid, so the launch never asks two cars that stay alongside to swap sides.
- **What sets the car size with the events data:** the 2025-07-09 entry puts four cars within 0.50 s (OpenAI, Google +0.15, Anthropic +0.15, xAI +0.20). That one pack makes every car about 20% smaller on the whole timeline (70 px long at 1440x900; the next tightest entry would allow 87). Opening it up a little, e.g. xAI +0.35, gives the size back. It is the research data's call, so it was left as delivered.
- **Logo boxes:** a LOGOS `box` is what gets centred and scaled into the roundel, and the roundel clips. The SpaceX AI box covers only the "XA", so its swoosh tail runs off the disc on purpose.
