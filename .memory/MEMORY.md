# Fastest Lab: project memory

- **index.html must stay one self-contained file.** The owner previews it in a viewer that opens index.html alone, so sibling files such as data.js never load. In Nov 2026 a separate data.js broke the page ("no usable snapshots"). The data now lives in the `<script id="race-data">` block near the top of index.html. Test changes with index.html copied alone into an empty folder.
- **Editing the race:** change the SNAPSHOTS text table in the DATA block. `gap` is seconds to the car directly ahead. The owner's scale: 1.0 = clear lead, 0.25 = wheel to wheel, 1.7 = big break. Labs and their rebrands go in LABS (`changes: [{from, name, sub, logo}]`), and logo SVG paths go in LOGOS.
- **Knobs:** the TUNING and PLAYBACK blocks just below DATA. `PLAYBACK.length` = seconds of driving (46; lights and launch come on top, about 53.5 s in all), `transition` [1.7, 2.8] and `hold` [1.1, 4] in seconds. `TUNING.zoom` (1.75) stretches tight fields to fill the height, `sideGap` (0.40 car widths) and `noseGap` (0.25 car lengths) are the room cars keep. `?speed=2` speeds up the replay. GT is the only car style since Nov 2026 (F1 and toy cars, the C key and `?cars=` are gone).
- **How the cars move (the drive plan):** nothing is simulated while it plays. `buildDrive()` works out, per screen size, a seat for every car at every snapshot and a path for every step between snapshots; a car's position is a pure function of the timeline position. The rule that makes "never more than three abreast" structural: two cars closer than a car length + noseGap along the road are at least a car width + sideGap apart across it, and the road is only three such lanes wide. The tightest four-car pack on the timeline caps the car size. Steps are planned in the background a few ms at a time (about 1 s in all on a laptop). `fastestLab.check()` in the console forces and measures every step; `ok: true` and `tight: 0` everywhere is the pass. New snapshots that pack four cars very closely make all cars smaller; a step that can't be driven cleanly is named in a console warning.
- **Logo boxes:** a LOGOS `box` is what gets centred and scaled into the roundel, and the roundel clips. The SpaceX AI box covers only the "XA", so its swoosh tail runs off the disc on purpose.
- **Background material** is in the parent folder ../:
  - BRIEF.md: the concept
  - research/standings-history.md: sources and judgement calls behind each snapshot
  - NAMES.md: parked name research
- **Verify headless** with Playwright `chromium.launch({channel:'chrome'})`; no bundled browsers are installed. Checked sizes: 1440x900 and 390x844 (plus 1920x1080, 1024x768, 844x390, 2560x1300, 360x640, 768x1024, 1280x720 for the drive plan).
