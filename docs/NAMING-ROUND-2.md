# Naming round 2

Concept: see BRIEF.md in this folder. A playful, opinionated, shareable one-page site ranking AI labs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta, SpaceX AI) by who has the best model lineup, shown as top-down F1-style race cars whose distances show the gaps, with a timeline slider that replays how the standings changed (overtakes and all).

## Where the owner stands after round 1

- **Fastest Lab** is still the favourite. It splits AI labs and racing about 50/50, and it makes sense as a phrase. Its one small flaw: "fastest" isn't quite the adjective you'd pick for the best lab. But "Best Lab" loses the racing link, so Fastest Lab still wins.
- **Lab by Lab** (from "lap by lap") is the runner-up. It's smart, just not quite as catchy.
- **Pole Model** was rejected: the pun is clever, but "a pole model" doesn't mean anything as a phrase. The owner is open to other ideas around **pole position**, as long as the result reads as a real phrase.
- **Formula AGI** grows on the owner but isn't good enough.
- Also seen and not chosen: Lab Prix, Model GP, Token GP, Model Drift, Neural Circuit, Checkered Flagship, Bench Racing, LLMans, Frontier GP, SOTA Grid, Overtake.ai.

## The bar

A name that (1) makes sense as a real phrase on its own, (2) carries both racing and AI labs/models roughly equally, (3) is short, catchy and easy to say out loud. One-letter or one-word twists of real racing phrases ("fastest lap" → "Fastest Lab", "lap by lap" → "Lab by Lab") are the direction that's working best.
