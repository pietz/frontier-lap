# Naming round 3

Concept: see BRIEF.md in this folder. A playful, opinionated, shareable one-page site ranking AI labs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta, SpaceX AI) by who has the best model lineup. The labs are top-down F1-style race cars, and the distances between them show the gaps. A timeline slider replays how the standings changed, overtakes and all. The hope is that it goes viral in the AI scene.

## What the owner thinks so far

- **Fastest Lab** (from "fastest lap") is still the favourite. It splits AI labs and racing about 50/50 and makes sense as a phrase. Its small flaw is that "fastest" isn't quite the right adjective for the best lab.
- **Victory Lab** (from "victory lap"): liked, but victorylab.com is a live app, so it's out.
- **Lab to Beat**: disliked at first, but it's slowly growing on the owner.
- **Lab by Lab** (from "lap by lap"): smart, but not quite catchy enough.
- **Rejected**: Pole Model (not a real phrase), Lab Leader, Formula AGI (close, but not good enough), Lead Lab, Lab Record, Lab Gap, Lab on Pole, P1 Lab, Labs on Track, A Lab Ahead, Model Race, Lab Replay, Lab Prix, Model GP, Token GP, Model Drift, Neural Circuit, Checkered Flagship, Bench Racing, LLMans, Frontier GP, SOTA Grid, Overtake.ai.

The owner feels the direction is right but there's more to find. Don't stop at one-letter lap→lab twists. Also explore:
- other racing vocabulary: grid, pit, paddock, podium, chequered flag, DRS, slipstream, apex, pit wall, team radio, parc fermé, quali, "box box", lights out
- AI vocabulary: model, weights, tokens, frontier, SOTA, benchmark, inference, AGI, compute
- shapes beyond two-word compounds

## Hard constraints

1. It makes sense as a real phrase on its own.
2. It carries racing and AI roughly equally.
3. It is short, catchy and easy to say out loud.
4. The **.com** must be unregistered. No .ai: too expensive. A cheap fitting TLD such as .net or .racing is a fallback only for a truly great name.
5. The domain must read cleanly as one word. Use a hyphen only if the run-together form misreads.

Check every .com before you propose it:
`curl -s -o /dev/null -w '%{http_code}' https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/NAME.com`
404 means unregistered, 200 means taken. Only propose names that return 404.
